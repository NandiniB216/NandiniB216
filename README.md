## Published research

**[Does earnings-call climate exposure improve stock downside-risk forecasts?](https://doi.org/10.7759/s44389-026-00359-6)**  
<sub>Published as <i>Does Earnings-Call Climate Exposure Improve Machine-Learning Forecasts of Stock Downside Risk? Out-of-Sample Evidence from U.S. Firms</i><br>Bhati N, Kataria A, Dhaundiyal P · Cureus Journal of Computer Science · 2026 · DOI 10.7759/s44389-026-00359-6</sub>

**Finding:** no. Across 1,792 U.S.-listed non-financial firms (15,521 firm-years), tested out of sample over 2017–2024, adding climate exposure left forecast accuracy essentially unchanged. A random forest was more accurate than linear models, but the advantage was only marginally significant, and most of its gain over a simple past-risk forecast came from the year after the COVID-19 shock.

**My role:** first author. I acquired, analysed and interpreted the data and ran the analyses. All three authors shared the study design and drafting, and my two co-authors supervised the work. As disclosed in the paper, an AI assistant (Claude) was used for literature searches and source verification, to write and debug the analysis code, and to draft and edit the text.

**Code and data:** [Replication files and analysis outputs (Zenodo)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23229046) · Software, version 3
